Where do you trade?

Fixed location with reliable internet? Go countertop. Moving around a venue? Portable. Out and about? Mobile.

How many terminals?

One terminal per till point is the rule of thumb. Add portables for table service or queue-busting during busy periods.

Terminals work standalone for simple payments. Add POS for inventory, staff management, reporting and more.
