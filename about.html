OUR STORY

The Stored story.

Customers find you on Instagram, in a WhatsApp chat or walking past your door, and want to pay right there. Stored started from a simple belief: wherever they find you, you should be able to take their payment, all through one account.

  1. 2022

    Two founders, one idea.

    Danny Howe and Lee Hart leave senior roles at WorldFirst (Alipay) and Clover. They’d both seen it: the way customers buy had changed, but payments hadn’t.

  2. 2023

    Starting with shoppers.

    The first Stored app lets shoppers save what they find on social media and buy it later. It shows us where buying really starts, and where payments let it down.

  3. 2024

    Turning to merchants.

    Hosted checkouts built for selling on social media and WhatsApp. We’re named in the Startups 100 and a finalist for London’s Fintech Startup of the Year.

  4. 2025

    Beyond social.

    Stored becomes an all-in-one, full-stack digital payments platform: pay by link, plus a virtual terminal for phone payments.

  5. 2026

    Worldpay partnership.

    Stored powers Worldpay’s payment links, reaching merchants who want to take payment however and wherever their customers find them.

  6. Today

    One account, one price.

    Card machines, EPOS, online checkout, payment links, invoicing and phone payments. Simple, transparent pricing: one flat rate.

  7. Coming soon

    Subscriptions.

    Set up and manage customer subscriptions on a regular billing cycle, so Stored covers every way your business gets paid, online and offline.

Why we exist.

Businesses deserve more. Today they juggle separate providers, channels and systems that don’t talk to each other. Our vision is one operating system for your business, where you get paid, manage your money and use your own data to make decisions that help you grow. Payments are where we started, but our commitment is we won’t just help you get paid. We will help you sell more.

THE FOUNDERS
Danny Howe

Eleven years at WorldFirst (Alipay), joining around employee 50 and rising to Managing Director on the exec team through its sale to Ant Group. He saw first hand how a fintech grows, and how much of that comes down to culture.

Lee Hart

Nine years at Clover (Fiserv), from SME Sales Director to General Manager and a seat on the board of First Data Europe. He knows what businesses need from a payments provider, and where they get let down.

They’d be the first to say they didn’t build Stored alone. Everyone who’s joined has shaped it.

Jonathan Quin

One of UK fintech’s success stories: he co-founded WorldFirst, grew it into a cross-border payments leader and sold it to Ant Group (Alipay) for around $650m. As Chairman, he’s backing Stored to do it again.
